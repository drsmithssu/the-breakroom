How to play
Guess the 5-letter nursing word in six tries. After each guess, the tiles change color: the filled dark tile means right letter in the right spot, the lighter gold tile means the letter is in the word but in a different spot, and gray means the letter is not in the word. Every answer is a word you'll meet in nursing school. Prefer stronger colors? Turn on High contrast below the puzzle number.
